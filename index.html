WHO WE ARE

In 2021, a budding automotive engineer (that's me!) helped a couple of friends on discord prepare their virtual cars for an upcoming e-sports competition, to try and apply his textbook learnings in the real (or rather virtual) world. The young engineer grew infatuated with vehicle dynamics and simulation, and got better and better at advising his friends, starting an informal consultancy.

Five years later in the summer of 2026, the not as young engineer graduated a Masters in Automotive Engineering, and his final year paper was proving a consumer grade racing simulator could emulate and mimic motorsport industry grade multibody handling models. His work on developing parametric DIL models on Assetto Corsa had netted him repeat international clients and his university's formula student team.

Big changes are now afoot for this humble consultancy outfit. A shiny new website is only the start. GARAGE-V is committed to applying engineering principles to consumer level racing simulators for a VERY flexible set of applications as per a potential client's needs.
