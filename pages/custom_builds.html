When I first worked on building up a one off assetto corsa model of a friend's autocross built mx-5, I wasn't planning on making this a regular offering from the not yet named GARAGE-V. Here we are several years later with international clients and close relationships, making vehicle models for driving simulators, and making them well!

My master's thesis laid the groundwork for this side of the consultancy, where I made an assetto corsa model of the Leeds Gryphon Racing formula student car and validated it against engineering spec multibody vehicle dynamics software.

What it would look like for you if you engage my services is you get a vehicle model to match your real life build and if you wished for it, a report authenticating its performance against ADAMS, CarMaker or Simulink.

You get a vehicle model on assetto corsa that you get to use without paying for track hire, tires and fuel. Past clients have used this to set design targets for future modifications, 'try on' different modifications before committing to a purchase, or for practice ahead of upcoming events.
