This is where it all began.

In 2021, sim racing was booming in popularity owing to Covid 19 taking away the opportunity for people to drive bits of metal and carbon fibre around in circles.

Young me tried out for Le Mans Virtual Series with the then shiny new Corvette Racing C8 GT3 class endurance racer - and wound up 5 seconds off the pace. I did however gain some good understanding of the platform, and used this to help an online friend out with their qualification attempt, which worked out great for them, qualifying for the finals!

Ever since, I've tried my hand at and broadened my range to encompass not just GT3 cars, but cup car setups, TCR, rally cars, drift cars and latest of all time-attack. Each discipline has exposed me to working with vastly differing machines and their associated communities.

What's very interesting with this work and the reason I've continued is that the data and telemetry might say a certain setup is ideal for fastest times - and then the driver calls the setup garbage. Having a quick car and maintaining driveability for unique driving styles is the challenge I love tackling.
